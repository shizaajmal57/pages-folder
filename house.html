About the House System

Since its introduction, the House System has helped schools promote personal responsibility and a sense of belonging among pupils. These smaller school communities encourage healthy rivalry through friendly games and extracurricular activities.

The system develops teamwork, leadership, cooperation, and individual strengths while building friendships among pupils of different age groups. Students join a House when they enter the school and remain members until they leave.

As students progress into senior classes, they take greater responsibility for managing their Houses and coordinating activities, helping them develop confidence, initiative, and organizational skills.

Purpose & Objectives

At Bahria College, the House System is designed to encourage participation, healthy competition, and a strong school community. The traditional school sports day is organized as an inter-house competition.

Teamwork

Working together, supporting peers, and achieving common goals.

Leadership

Developing confidence, responsibility, and decision-making skills.

Healthy Competition

Encouraging fair play, participation, and continuous improvement.

School Spirit

Building a strong sense of community and school pride.

Community Service

Promoting service to others and a sense of social responsibility.

Personal Growth

Recognizing individual skills and learning to handle success and failure.

House Structure

Students are assigned to Houses according to their performance, ability, and talents, with the aim of maintaining a reasonable balance between Houses and encouraging fair competition.
